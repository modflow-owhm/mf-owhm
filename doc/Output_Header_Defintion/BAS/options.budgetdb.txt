
BAS Package Options block keyword BUDGETDB

Writes for each time step the volumetric budget 
for the entire model domain. This is equivalent 
to the LIST files volumetric budget with the header:

VOLUMETRIC BUDGET FOR ENTIRE MODEL AT END OF TIME STEP    x IN STRESS PERIOD      y

except that: 
   (1) All values are rates, and 
   (2) Each row represents a time step and each column is the 
          budget name (MODFLOW package) with _IN and _OUT appended to the end.
   (3) IN_OUT represents the rate version of "TOTAL IN" minus "TOTAL OUT"
          Defined as:
                     IN_OUT = "TOTAL IN" - "TOTAL OUT"
          It is positive when more water enters than leaves the model and negative otherwise.
   (4) PERCENT_ERROR represents the absolute value of the "PERCENT DISCREPANCY" (always positive)
          Defined as:
                     AVE_FLOW      = ("TOTAL IN" + "TOTAL OUT") / 2
                     PERCENT_ERROR = 100 * abs(IN_OUT) / AVE_FLOW
          If AVE_FLOW is zero (less than 1E-30), then PERCENT_ERROR is set to zero.
          If both abs(IN_OUT) and AVE_FLOW are less than 0.001, then PERCENT_ERROR is limited to 0.001.
   (5) ITER is the number of outer iterations required to solve the time step.
   (6) HCLOSE    [L]    is the maximum absolute head change of the last outer iteration, ITER.
                          That is, HCLOSE = max( abs( HNEW(ITER) - HNEW(ITER-1) ) )
                          This is the "HClose" value in the LIST file's "Solver Convergence Information".
   (7) L2_RCLOSE [L3/T] is the L2 norm (square root of the sum of the squares) of the
                          flow residual error of each active model cell for the last outer iteration, ITER.
                          This is the "L2-RClose" value in the LIST file's "Solver Convergence Information".
   (8) ITER, HCLOSE, and L2_RCLOSE are zero for stress periods that are
          not simulated (BAS options INPUT_CHECK or FASTFORWARD).

For example, the following is printed to the LIST file:
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  VOLUMETRIC BUDGET FOR ENTIRE MODEL AT END OF TIME STEP    1 IN STRESS PERIOD      1
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

     CUMULATIVE VOLUMES      L**3       RATES FOR THIS TIME STEP      L**3/T
     ------------------                 ------------------------

           IN:                                      IN:
           ---                                      ---
             STORAGE =       18510.0000               STORAGE =        1234.0000
       CONSTANT HEAD =           0.0000         CONSTANT HEAD =           0.0000

            TOTAL IN =       18510.0000              TOTAL IN =        1234.0000

          OUT:                                     OUT:
          ----                                     ----
             STORAGE =         765.0000               STORAGE =          51.0000
       CONSTANT HEAD =        7500.0000         CONSTANT HEAD =         500.0000

           TOTAL OUT =        8265.0000             TOTAL OUT =         551.0000

            IN - OUT =       10245.0000              IN - OUT =         683.0000

 PERCENT DISCREPANCY =          76.53     PERCENT DISCREPANCY =          76.53
 
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Date output is formatted using ISO 8601-1:2019 standard without a time zone designation:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601#Combined_date_and_time_representations

The general format is:
yyyy-mm-ddThh:mm:ss
                   where:
                         yyyy is the four digit Gregorian year
                         mm   before the T is the two digit month number       (01 to 12)
                         dd   is the two digit day of the month                (01 to {28, 29, 30, 31}, depending on the month)
                         T    calendar date and 24-hour clock time separator
                         hh   is the hour within the day                       (00 to 23 hour)
                         mm   after the T is the two digit minutes in the hour (00 to 59 minute)
                         ss   is the two digit seconds of the minute           (00 to 59 second)

Output is in model units that uses an
   L as a place holder for Length, L2 is for area, L3 is for volume and
   T as a place holder for Time

which are defined in the DIS package as being:
   L: Feet, Meters, Centimeters, and Undefined
   T: Seconds, Minutes, Hours, Days, Years, and Undefined
